Questions

Before you buy.

Is 500 ANSI lumens bright enough for my room?−
In a dark or dim room, yes — comfortably, up to about 120 inches. In a bright room with daylight on the wall, every projector at this size struggles; 500 true ANSI is roughly what a 2,000 "LED lumen" budget projector actually measures.
Do I need a streaming stick?+
No. Android 14 is built in.
How far back do I need to sit?+
The throw ratio is 1:1 — roughly another 40 inches of screen for every metre you step back, from 40" at 0.9m to 200" at 5m.
