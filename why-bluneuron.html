Designed to be trusted. Built to stay.

Bluneuron exists on a simple conviction: the things you choose to bring into your home should feel considered, not just purchased.

Why we exist

The market deserves better than "cheap and disposable."

We started Bluneuron with one question — what would it take to make something people are genuinely glad they own, years after the first day?

People are used to treating gadgets in this price range as disposable: buy cheap, expect it to fail, replace it. We think that's a low bar, and a needlessly expensive one for the people paying it. The products you live with every day shouldn't be the ones you trust least.

So every Bluneuron product is judged on the same test before it ships: will this still feel like a good decision two years from now? If the honest answer is no, it isn't ready.

What we hold ourselves to

Three pillars, each with a proof point — not just an adjective.

01Trust

We tell you what we know and what we're still validating. The Mini Projector page says plainly where the product is made and how it's checked. No inflated claims, no vanishing after checkout — a 2-year warranty and support that answers.

02Intelligent design

Auto-Focus. Auto Keystone. An integrated 360° gimbal stand instead of a box you store separately. A chrome-rimmed lens with a cover that slides shut on its own. Every design choice removes a step you'd otherwise have to think about.

03Dependable quality

Every unit is checked for focus accuracy, brightness consistency and build tolerance before it reaches you. If something's wrong, we own it and we fix it. Reliability over flash.

Sourcing & quality process

Transparency builds more trust than silence.

Our products are manufactured with established partners in China and quality-checked before they're cleared for shipment. We inspect on every batch — not a sample here and there — and we'd rather say that out loud than let you guess.

Owning the sourcing and the checks around it is part of the point. A premium experience has to be true before it's written on a page.

Specific inspection steps and certifications will be published here as they are finalised.

If something goes wrong, here's what happens.

Reach us on WhatsApp or by email — no store visit needed. Faulty on arrival: a no-questions replacement within 7 days. After that: 2 years of warranty coverage, with a typical turnaround of 5–7 business days. We reply to support messages within 24 hours. Go to Support
