Product — Mini Projector

Movie night. A big match. Your favorite show, but bigger.

The Bluneuron Mini Projector turns any flat wall into a screen up to 120 inches — and sets itself up while you're still finding a comfortable seat.

Design & build

Finished to be seen, not hidden away.

Finished in clean matte white, the Mini Projector is built to sit on a shelf like something you chose on purpose — not something you're hiding when guests come over. It folds onto its own compact stand, angles freely, and at 1.35 kg, moves from the living room to the terrace to a friend's place without a second thought. Every curve and vent is there for a reason: quiet cooling, a stable base, a lens that's protected when it's not in use.

Performance, in plain language

Sets itself up. Shows up sharp.

  • Sharp, real detail. Native Full HD (1920×1080) — not upscaled, not stretched.
  • Sets itself up. Auto focus and auto keystone correct blur and angle distortion automatically.
  • Bright enough for real rooms. ~300 ANSI lumens through a 2.69" high-lumens optical engine.
  • Scales with your space. 40 inches for a small room, up to 120 inches for a home-theatre feel.
  • Smart, not stripped-down. Android 14 with 2GB+16GB storage — install your own streaming apps.
  • Quiet enough to forget it's there. 32 dB operating noise.

Quality & testing

Owning the process, not hiding it.

Every Bluneuron Mini Projector passes rigorous quality inspection before it's cleared to ship. We test focus accuracy, brightness consistency, and build tolerance on every batch — a premium experience has to be true before it's ever written on a page. This matters even more given the product is sourced from China: owning that sourcing, and the QC around it, builds more trust than silence does.
